Subject: WolfGrid — Canadian historical hail footprint sample and archive access

WolfGrid helps roofing teams find past hailstorms, select affected neighbourhoods, count existing address pins, and create field-sales campaigns.

We need one real Alberta historical hail event from October 11, 2025 through October 11, 2026 as an initial sample, followed by the previous 12 months of available Alberta events.

Please provide:
- A dated event identifier and actual hail-size grid or Polygon/MultiPolygon GeoJSON, not an image, point-location history, or forecast warning area.
- Numerical estimated hail sizes in millimetres, or cumulative polygons for >=25.4 mm, >=44.45 mm, and >=63.5 mm.
- Coordinate reference system, native resolution, estimation method, observed period, source update time, coverage/no-data mask, and uncertainty information.
- A documented event search/export endpoint or downloadable sample, authentication instructions, request costs, historical retention, regional coverage, and update frequency.
- Written confirmation that WolfGrid may store this data, display it to paying customers, and calculate address exposure from its geometry. Storage permission has already been reported by our team; please confirm the remaining permitted uses and attribution requirements.

The first acceptance test is one real historical severity overlay, independently checked unique address counts, and one territory-to-campaign handoff. We will not represent estimated exposure as confirmed roof damage.

Xweather: Can our existing account access this storm-wide Canadian historical product? The documented /hail/archive location histories and recent MapsGL hail-size layer do not establish that access. If a separate product/export arrangement is required, please specify it.

Alternative supplier: The Weather Company HailVision documents Canadian MESH grids and threshold polygons. Please confirm whether the previous 12 months are exportable, rather than only the current observation products.
